FAQ

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Profile → Manage Subscription. That opens Stripe's billing portal, where you can cancel anytime — you keep access through the end of the period you've already paid for, and your logged data is never affected either way.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Settings → Delete Account, type DELETE to confirm. This is immediate and permanent — it removes your profile, logs, photos, and subscription, in-app, without needing to email anyone.

What data does Krafft send to AI, and can I turn it off?

Only after you agree to it — Krafft shows a one-time consent screen before the first AI feature you use (Describe It, Snap a Photo, Ask AI, Session Analysis), explaining exactly what's sent. You can withdraw that consent anytime from Profile → AI Features, which turns every AI feature back off immediately.

I upgraded on iPhone — why did it open my browser?

Subscriptions are billed through Krafft's website via Stripe, so "Upgrade" opens that checkout page in your browser rather than inside the app. Once you finish, return to Krafft — your Pro status updates automatically within about 30 seconds.

I never got my confirmation email.

Check your spam folder first. If it's not there, double-check the email address you signed up with was typed correctly, then contact us and we'll help directly.
