Krafft training redesign: the whole flow

Everything we picked, together in one walk-through: Today, Plans hub (1A), editable "your version" splits (B1, B2, B3), the muscle-first exercise screen (A2) inside the improved workout flow (3A + A3), session analysis, and the exercise library with history, form and swaps (2A). It uses the same look as the live app (cream, brass, Fraunces / Inter / IBM Plex Mono), the 3-tab bar and gold + button. Sample numbers only.

Body map: the figures in this mockup are placeholders. In the build, every diagram uses the app's existing muscle map from the website, with the same look. Only the highlighting (brass = primary muscle, lighter = secondary) and the sizing are new.
Design tokens: cream #F4F2ED · card #FBFAF7 · ink #17181B · muted #77756F · line #E4E0D6 · brass #8A6A3A · gold #C7A15E · ok #4C7A5A · warn #B5532F. Glass tab bar rgba(23,24,27,.86), blur 14px. Minimum tap target 44pt. Numbers in IBM Plex Mono.

Rules that hold across every screen

Editing: always your own copy; original untouched; Undo on every removal; Reset to original; preview before changing days per week. Reliability: every set saves the instant it's checked, the rest timer survives backgrounding and a locked screen, an interrupted workout resumes exactly where it left off. History safety: swapping or removing an exercise never changes past logged sessions. Reading: max 3 chips visible at a time, numbers in mono at 13pt or larger, color only for state. Free vs Pro: everything on these screens is free except the full session analysis (AI) and anything already Pro. On iPhone, the Pro row opens the in-app purchase paywall.
